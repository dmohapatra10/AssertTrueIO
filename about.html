ABOUT ASSERTTRUEIO

Learn testing the
way it's actually done.

AssertTrueIO is a free, hands-on library of automation testing tutorials — built for engineers who want to understand not just the syntax, but why a real test suite is built the way it is.

Why this site exists

Most tutorials teach a tool's API surface — here's a locator, here's an assertion, here's how to click a button. That's necessary, but it's not the same as knowing how to build something that survives contact with a real, messy application and a real CI pipeline. AssertTrueIO exists to close that gap: every chapter goes past "here's the syntax" into why it's designed that way, what actually breaks in practice, and what a production-style framework built around it looks like.

What you'll find here

Four complete, self-paced tracks, covering the full automation engineering toolkit:

  • Playwright — 14 chapters, 63 lessons, from your first test to a production-grade end-to-end framework.
  • RestAssured — 15 chapters, 77 lessons, covering API test automation with Java from HTTP fundamentals through framework architecture and CI/CD.
  • K6 — 14 chapters, 55 lessons, covering performance testing from core concepts through a production-style framework.
  • GitHub Actions — 14 chapters, 68 lessons, covering CI/CD from first principles through a real, production-grade deployment pipeline.

How each chapter is built

Every lesson on this site follows the same discipline, deliberately:

  • What the concept actually is, and why it exists
  • A basic example, then a detailed, realistic one
  • The common mistakes engineers actually make with it
  • Edge cases and debugging guidance drawn from real failures
  • A set of interview questions — the same kind you'd genuinely be asked

The goal isn't just to get a test passing. It's to leave you able to explain your choices to a teammate, or in an interview, with confidence.

About the author

AssertTrueIO is written and maintained by Debashish Mohapatra, a QA Automation Consultant Lead (SDET) whose work spans test automation, framework architecture, and technical content creation. He holds an AI-Enabled Engineer certification from Saksoft LTD, and has authored six books on Amazon covering Playwright and RestAssured — from complete beginner guides to dedicated interview-preparation editions.

Everything published as a book started here first, as a free tutorial. The books exist for people who want the same material end-to-end, offline, or on Kindle — not as a paywall on top of the site.
