CONTRIBUTE

Help make this
site better.

AssertTrueIO is maintained by one person, and it improves mainly because readers point out what's wrong or missing. There's no submission form or repo to open a pull request against — just send an email, and real, specific feedback gets acted on.

What's worth sending

  • An actual error — a code example that doesn't run, a wrong explanation, a broken link, an outdated API reference.
  • A gap — a scenario you hit in real work that a chapter doesn't cover, or doesn't cover deeply enough.
  • A topic request — something you think deserves its own chapter that isn't here yet.
  • A confusing explanation — if a concept didn't click even after reading the "why," that's useful to know.

What makes an email actionable

The faster a report can be verified and fixed, the faster it actually gets fixed. Where you can, include:

  • The exact page (a URL, or the chapter and lesson name)
  • What's wrong, or what you expected instead
  • For a code issue — what you ran, and what actually happened

Thanks — that's on its way.

Real, specific reports get read and acted on. You'll hear back if it needs any follow-up.

Sent via a form relay, not a public inbox address — reduces spam without a backend of my own.
