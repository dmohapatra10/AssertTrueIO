Why This Chapter Is Both New and Familiar

Chapter 8 touched data privacy. Chapter 11 insisted on human approval. Chapter 13 built safety controls. Chapter 15 covered MCP security. This chapter gathers that scattered discipline into one place — and adds something genuinely new none of those pages addressed: an agent that autonomously browses real pages, the way Chapter 13 and 14's agents do, can encounter content on those pages actively trying to manipulate it.
