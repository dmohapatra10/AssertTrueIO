Why It Matters — This Is Genuinely the Whole Track, Connected

Every arrow in this diagram is a real handoff this track has built before, just never all in the same continuous run. Chapter 4's generated test case becomes Chapter 5's executed plan. Chapter 5's execution produces Chapter 9's evidence. Chapter 9's evidence feeds Chapter 9's classification and Chapter 12's flaky check. A genuine defect triggers Chapter 9's root cause and Chapter 11's fix. Chapter 11's validation confirms the fix actually works. Chapter 14's honest evaluation discipline closes the loop with a final, trustworthy report.
