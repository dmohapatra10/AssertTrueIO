Common Mistakes

  • Treating this chapter as a bigger version of Chapter 5's loop. The real difference is genuine open-endedness — no fixed sequence exists to fall back on, which changes what "the next action" even means.
  • Letting exploration run unbounded. This chapter's fourth and seventh pages exist specifically because open-ended exploration needs real, explicit limits and safety controls from the start.

Best Practices

  • Read this chapter in order — strategy, observation, limits, prioritization, and hypothesis testing all build on each other.
  • Keep Chapter 5's Observation-to-Next-Action loop in mind as the genuine foundation this chapter extends, not replaces.

Key Takeaways

  • Exploratory testing means genuinely open-ended objectives, with no predetermined sequence of steps to follow.
  • This is a real extension of Chapter 5's observation loop, not a different technique — the difference is what decides the next action.
  • Genuine open-endedness requires real, explicit limits and safety controls, covered directly in this chapter, not left implicit.
