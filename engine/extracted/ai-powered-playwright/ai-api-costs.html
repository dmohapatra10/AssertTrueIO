Introduction

Cost is genuinely simple arithmetic — tokens used, multiplied by price per token. This page is about applying that arithmetic honestly to real techniques from this track, without hardcoding specific dollar figures that would be stale within months of being written.

How It Works — The Real, Simple Formula

cost = (input_tokens × input_price_per_token) +
       (output_tokens × output_price_per_token)

Real, CURRENT pricing is published at
https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/about-claude/pricing — worth
checking directly rather than trusting a number written into a
tutorial, since pricing genuinely changes over time.

Example — Estimating a Real Monthly Total

Why It Matters — This Kind of Estimate Is the Real Point

The specific dollar total isn't what matters here — current pricing at the link above answers that directly, and it changes. What matters is the real, durable exercise: counting actual call volume across a real suite's actual scale, the same way this example did. This is what turns "AI calls cost money" from a vague worry into a concrete, plannable number.

Common Mistakes

  • Hardcoding a specific dollar figure into planning documents or code comments. Pricing changes — a written-down number becomes actively misleading the moment it goes stale, worse than no number at all.
  • Estimating cost from token count alone, ignoring call volume. A cheap individual call, multiplied by thousands of real executions per month, can still add up to a genuinely significant total.

Best Practices

  • Always check current, real pricing directly from the source rather than trusting a number written into any tutorial, including this one.
  • Estimate real monthly cost using actual call volume at your own suite's genuine scale, the way this page's example does.
