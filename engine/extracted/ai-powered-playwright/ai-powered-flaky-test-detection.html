Common Mistakes

  • Labeling any single failure "flaky" without checking real history. This chapter's early pages are explicit that genuine flakiness requires evidence across multiple runs, not a one-off guess.
  • Treating "flaky" as a permanent label to route around. This chapter's closing page is explicit about why suppression without genuine investigation is a real, costly mistake.

Best Practices

  • Read this chapter in order — the trace-diffing technique in its middle pages depends on the history collection built earlier.
  • Keep Chapter 10's trace extraction functions in mind — this chapter reuses them directly, rather than building new extraction logic.

Key Takeaways

  • Genuine flakiness detection requires real history across multiple runs, not a judgment from a single failure.
  • Diffing a passing trace against a failing one from the same test is a genuinely Playwright-specific technique this chapter builds on Chapter 10's foundation.
  • This chapter closes with why suppressing a flaky test, rather than genuinely investigating it, is a real, costly mistake — not a reasonable shortcut.
