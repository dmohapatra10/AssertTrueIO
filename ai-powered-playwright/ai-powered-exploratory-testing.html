AI-Powered Exploratory Testing

Beginner 2 min read

Prerequisites: AI-Powered Flaky Test Detection

What You'll Learn

  • Why an open-ended exploration objective is genuinely different from Chapter 5's fixed, known sequence
  • Deciding what to explore next, prioritized by real risk, not arbitrary order
  • Generating and testing genuine hypotheses about unexpected behavior, with real limits and safety controls

Seven Pages, One Genuinely Open-Ended Objective

  1. What is AI Exploratory Testing?
  2. AI Exploration Strategy
  3. Browser Observation
  4. Exploration Limits
  5. Risk-Based Exploration
  6. Bug Discovery
  7. Safety Controls

The Objective This Chapter Builds Toward

Explore the checkout functionality
and identify unexpected behaviour.

Compare this to Chapter 5's instruction — "login, search, select, add to cart, verify total" had five known steps before anything ran. This objective has none. The agent genuinely has to decide what "explore checkout" means, moment to moment, based on what it actually discovers.

Why This Is Genuinely Different From Chapter 5

CHAPTER 5: a KNOWN sequence, broken into STEPS in advance —
  the Observation → Next Action loop existed to HANDLE
  dependent steps within an OTHERWISE predetermined path

THIS CHAPTER: NO predetermined path at ALL — the agent must
  GENERATE its own next action, EVERY time, based on what
  it's ALREADY discovered, with genuine OPEN-ENDEDNESS about
  where exploration LEADS

Common Mistakes

  • Treating this chapter as a bigger version of Chapter 5's loop. The real difference is genuine open-endedness — no fixed sequence exists to fall back on, which changes what "the next action" even means.
  • Letting exploration run unbounded. This chapter's fourth and seventh pages exist specifically because open-ended exploration needs real, explicit limits and safety controls from the start.

Best Practices

  • Read this chapter in order — strategy, observation, limits, prioritization, and hypothesis testing all build on each other.
  • Keep Chapter 5's Observation-to-Next-Action loop in mind as the genuine foundation this chapter extends, not replaces.

Key Takeaways

  • Exploratory testing means genuinely open-ended objectives, with no predetermined sequence of steps to follow.
  • This is a real extension of Chapter 5's observation loop, not a different technique — the difference is what decides the next action.
  • Genuine open-endedness requires real, explicit limits and safety controls, covered directly in this chapter, not left implicit.
